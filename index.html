About the studio

Hi! I'm Aes, the illustrator behind Lazy Tale. I've been drawing stories since 2015, and I started this studio so every story can get the cover and the world it deserves.

Today we work with authors, small presses and creators around the world, all from Indonesia. We keep a small number of projects at a time, so each one gets real attention.

FAQ

Good questions

Can't find your answer here? Just send us a message.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the piece. A portrait is quick; a full wrap cover or a comic takes longer. We take a few projects at a time because quality comes first, and we agree on a timeline with you before we start.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted on its own, based on format, detail and deadline. Send a short brief and we'll reply with a price. Need it sooner? Rush delivery is available for an extra fee.

What do you draw?

Humans, creatures, monsters and landscapes, including darker themes like blood, scars and wounds. We don't take NSFW work, political or ideological pieces, or portraits of real people.

Do you work with clients outside Indonesia?

Yes. We work remotely in English with authors and publishers all over the world.
